Reference

Glycemic index of foods

The menu’s own foods first, with their carbs per serving, then the 150-food reference. Weigh one, or carry its GI up into the calculator.

The one formula

Glycemic load = GI × net carbs ÷ 100

Net carbs are the total carbohydrate minus its fiber — the carbohydrate that can raise blood glucose. A load of 10 or less is low, 10 to 20 medium, 20 or more high, for one serving.

Bands

Reading the numbers

Glycemic index

How fast

Against pure glucose at 100, tested on a fixed amount of carbohydrate.

  • 55 or less · low
  • 56–69 · medium
  • 70 or more · high

A property of the food, not the portion: a slow rise means steadier energy and less insulin demand; a fast one, a spike and a dip.

Glycemic load

How much

The index scaled by the net carbs actually eaten.

  • 10 or less · low
  • 10–20 · medium
  • 20 or more · high

A high-GI food eaten in a small amount can still carry a low load. The bands are set for one serving, so a whole meal reads strictly.

Settings

Count ityour way.

Six choices, saved in this browser. Change any of them and every number on the page follows.

What counts

Your numbers, your care team’s targets.

Carb counting, exchange units and a meal budget come first; the rest set the daily references the totals are measured against.

  1. 01

    Carbs to count

    The number the readout and the totals lead with. Most carb counting uses the label’s Total Carbohydrate. Net carbs take the fiber off, which is also what glycemic load is built on.

  2. 02

    Carb choices

    Shows the count in exchange units as well as grams. In the US one carb choice is 15 g.

  3. 03

    Carb budget for a meal

    If your care team has given you a carbohydrate target for each meal, enter it and the plate is measured against it. Leave it empty for none.

    g per meal

  4. 04

    Count by

    Whether the plate takes servings or a weight from a kitchen scale. Either way each food names what one serving weighs and how to weigh it — oats dry, fruit peeled.

  5. 05

    Your body weight

    Sets the protein target at 0.8 g per kg. Leave it empty for the default of 64 g, an 80 kg (177 lb) adult.

  6. 06

    Added sugar limit

    The American Heart Association’s daily maximum: 36 g for men, 25 g for women.

Privacy

Kept in this browser, nowhere else.

Your settings and plate are saved under meal.settings and meal.plate in this browser’s own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing the browser’s site data clears them.
