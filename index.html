Settings

Carbs to count

The number the readout and Totals lead with. Most carb counting uses the label’s Total Carbohydrate. Net carbs take the fiber off, which is also what glycemic load is built on.

Carb choices

Shows the count in exchange units as well as grams. In the US one carb choice is 15 g.

Carb budget for a meal

If your care team has given you a carbohydrate target per meal, enter it and the readout measures the plate against it. Leave it empty for none.

Count by

Whether the plate takes servings or a weight from a kitchen scale. Either way each food says what one serving weighs and how to weigh it — oats dry, fruit peeled.

Your body weight

Sets the protein target at 0.8 g per kg. Leave it empty to use the default of 64 g, which is an 80 kg (177 lb) adult.

Added sugar limit

The American Heart Association’s daily maximum is 36 g for men and 25 g for women.

Install as an app

Kept on a home screen or in a dock, it opens in its own window and works with no connection.

Where this is kept

Your settings and plate are saved in this browser only, under meal.settings and meal.plate. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser’s site data clears them.

What these numbers use

Everything above with a * is a setting rather than a fixed rule: change it under Settings.
